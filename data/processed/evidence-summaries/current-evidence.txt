Current preliminary evidence

Hardware:
- Physical Ubuntu system
- AMD Ryzen 7 7445HS
- 6 physical cores
- 12 logical CPUs
- 16 MiB L3 cache
- perf hardware counters accessible

Workloads:
- sequential
- strided
- random
- hash
- transition

Datasets:
- seed 11: 15 paired observations
- seed 17: 15 paired observations
- total: 30 paired observations

Preliminary validation:
- Combined leave-one-pattern-out improvement: 47.88%
- Seed-11 held-out improvement: 70.72%
- Seed-17 held-out improvement: 66.44%

Interpretation:
Affine calibration reduced preliminary hardware-versus-Cachegrind
prediction error on held-out workload patterns and independent seed datasets.

Limitations:
- Hardware and Cachegrind executions were sequentially paired, not simultaneous.
- Only one physical CPU has been tested.
- perf generic cache events and Cachegrind D1 events are related but not identical.
- Quality filtering and final untouched holdout evaluation remain pending.
